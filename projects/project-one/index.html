Personal Budget Dashboard

A weekly dashboard I built with Claude Cowork to track my rent, paychecks, groceries, and savings in one place.

Updated

The Motivation

Going into my master's year, I am balancing a part-time job, TA income, and rent for my apartment. I needed an easy way to keep myself accountable for budgeting for groceries and everyday spending, and to plan ahead with my savings.

How It Works

Every Sunday, I upload the week's transactions and the dashboard sorts them into categories, tracks my grocery budget for the month, and shows what to set aside from each paycheck for rent and bills. Whatever is left gets split between savings and fun money.

How I Built It

I built it through a mix of vibe coding and teaching myself how to use Claude Code on my own, along with some research into the best way to post the dashboard and keep it up to date.

To stay consistent, I set up a weekly push notification that reminds me on my phone to upload my spending from the previous week. As I keep adding to it, I fine-tune the dashboard as needed.
