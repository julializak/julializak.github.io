A Milwaukee Must

Brew Crew Summer

You can’t spend a summer in Milwaukee without becoming a Brewers fan, and Rockwell made sure of it.

Between intern outings, IT team nights, and Rockwell-wide events, I spent a lot of my summer at American Family Field. Baseball is woven into this city. On game days the whole town shows up in navy and gold, and the ballpark becomes the place everyone wants to be.

Some of my favorite conversations of the summer happened in the stands, not in a conference room. Going to games with other interns, my IT team, and people from all across the company was one of the easiest ways to get to know everyone outside of work.

And honestly? They’re playing so well right now. What a year to start becoming a Brewers fan.

Go Brew Crew!
