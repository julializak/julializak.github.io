The Move

A new city, a new team

In May 2025, I packed up my life in Indiana and moved to a completely new city and state, Dallas, Texas, without even getting to go home to Michigan after school. Here is my story about a new city, a new team, and my first internship.

Just 24 hours after getting back from Golf Nationals 2025 in Williamsburg, Virginia, my dad and I packed up my college dorm and started our trip to Dallas on Saturday. By Monday, I was starting my first day of work.

The Role

MIS Data Intern

I worked in the Technology department, supporting teams across the business by pulling and presenting data to answer real sales and business questions.

Mind, Body & Soul

Finding my love for yoga

One of the most unexpected parts of my summer in Texas was falling in love with yoga. What started as something new to try quickly became part of my routine and something I looked forward to every week.

It taught me how important it is to take care of your mind, body, and soul, especially during a summer full of so many new things. Making time to slow down and reset helped me show up as my best self, both at work and outside of it.
