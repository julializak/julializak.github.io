Never Stop Learning

A one-line summary of what this article is about.

As I walked into a coffee shop this morning to get some work done and do some self-learning, this wall immediately caught my eye.

"Never Stop Learning."

Very simple, but it hit me. It is truly a great reminder to receive as a young woman early in her career. I am constantly weighing what I want to do long-term. Trying to stay current on new skills, technology, platforms. Trying to figure out what's actually worth my time and focus.

Every day, I learn more and more about the power of taking the extra time to watch a short YouTube video, meet and learn from someone experienced, or read an article to learn something new, instead of scrolling on your phone during your free time. This makes a real difference over time.

Continuing to learn only makes you better, stronger, more valuable, and I strive to continue doing that.

What's one small thing you do to keep learning outside of work? Any advice on resources, articles, focus points, books? Let me know!
