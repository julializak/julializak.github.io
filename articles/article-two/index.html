What Product Market Fit Taught Me

A reflection on taking Notre Dame's ESTEEM Product Market Fit course as a computer science student, and the other side of technology it opened up.

When I reached out to ask if I could join the ESTEEM program's Product Market Fit course as an MSCSE student, I wrote that I thought it "could be one of the most formative things I do before I start my career."

It was.

I was the only non-ESTEEM student in the room. Thank you to the ESTEEM program for letting me take the class, and to the teaching team for all their support this semester.

Most of all, thank you to our professor and Unusual Ventures. The class didn't just teach a process. It gave us a whole new way of thinking, and I'm so grateful I got to learn it.

The other side of technology

As much as I love the technical side, I keep finding myself drawn to the other half of it: what people actually want, and how a business and its users can see the same product from completely different angles. This class showed me that other side. I got to see how a product comes to be, and how much grit and discipline it takes to get there.

One of my goals is to gain experience and knowledge across every side of companies and technology. My biggest takeaway is that the technology on its own isn't enough. You have to understand the people you're building for and what they need. Once you do, it changes the way you work.

I'm looking forward to bringing this into my work, my life, and the way I think. I'm also grateful for all the new relationships I made along the way.
